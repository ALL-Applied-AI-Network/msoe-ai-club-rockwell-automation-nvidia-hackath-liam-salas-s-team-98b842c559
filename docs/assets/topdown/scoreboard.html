Tracker protocol comparison: where the coverage gap comes from

The frozen tracker starts a track only in the entry band, so a cap whose track died during a long dropout in the station holder is never re-born and its later detections are discarded: dense53 finds 92% of cap points per frame but only 65% reach a camera ID. A variant declared before scoring lets an unclaimed lane box start a track anywhere in the lane, provisional until three accepted observations and never inheriting a lost ID. Every model's saved detections were replayed through both trackers with the frozen scorer; the frozen rows reproduce the harness reports exactly. The frozen protocol stays the headline; this is a labelled comparison, with its costs.

How it works

Synthetic twin renders5160 training and 1280 validation frames of the station at 640 x 480, each with a per-cap instance mask. No real frame is ever loaded by the trainer.
Augmentation for the real worldHue, blur, noise and JPEG jitter; non-cap patches, dark polylines and light rectangles pasted into the lane as negatives; synthetic caps pasted onto that structure and onto drawn dark holder squares as positives.
DenseCapNet U-NetWidths 12 / 24 / 48 / 96, 275k parameters, GroupNorm, trained with BCE (positive weight 50) plus Dice, caps weighted by instance area. Output is one cap-probability map at native resolution.
Components and confidencePixels above 0.5 become 8-connected components of at least 9 px. Each gets a box, a mask and a confidence equal to its mean probability.
Synthetic-derived floorComponents below a floor are dropped. The floor is the 1st percentile of true-cap confidence on synthetic validation, rounded down to 0.05, fixed before any real scoring. Dense53: 0.90.
Tracker and lineThe fixed guarded tracker turns per-frame caps into camera IDs with states (visible, lost, ambiguous), lane entries, exits and reference-line crossings, which is what ties measurements to the right vial downstream.
Checkpoints are selected on synthetic validation Dice (or, for the EMA family, the final averaged checkpoint). Per-epoch real numbers were computed only as diagnostics, never to pick a model.
