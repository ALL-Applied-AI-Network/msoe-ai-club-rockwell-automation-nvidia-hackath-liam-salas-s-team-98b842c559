## Summary

- First vial after a recipe change (n=8): distance to the settled colour median 22.9 (range 3.5 to 50.4); the deviation points at the previous recipe with direction cosine median 0.97, 5 of 8 at or above 0.9; fitted share of the previous recipe median 0.68 (range 0.27 to 1.63).
- Size of the recipe change versus first-vial error (n=8): rank correlation 0.72 (Spearman on the table's one-decimal values; 0.69 on the unrounded values); 6 of 8 first vials deviate less than their recipe gap, vials 16, 25 exceed it. A reading of the table, not a fitted law.
- Second vial of the run (n=10): distance to the settled colour median 2.3 (range 0.7 to 15.3). Attributed toward the recipe that preceded the run (previous column shows it): vial 5 dE 15.3 cosine 0.99 share 0.29; vial 8 dE 0.8 cosine 0.22 share 0.00; vial 11 dE 2.0 cosine 0.07 share 0.00; vial 14 dE 2.6 cosine -0.94 share -0.37; vial 17 dE 0.7 cosine 0.73 share 0.04; vial 20 dE 1.6 cosine -0.38 share -0.02; vial 23 dE 4.4 cosine 0.94 share 0.07; vial 26 dE 1.1 cosine -0.82 share -0.05; vial 29 dE 8.2 cosine 0.69 share 0.16. The second vials that still deviate by more than a few units point at the pre-run recipe (residual carryover, decaying), while the small deviations point in no particular direction (noise).
- A share above 1 means the vial looks even more like the previous recipe than that recipe's own settled vial; with its single position-3 vial (n=1 per recipe) as the settled colour and uncalibrated camera colour, shares are indicative, not volumetric.
- Reading: the colour error of the first vial after a changeover is explained by the previous recipe, which is the carryover signature; by the second vial it has mostly decayed. The exception is recipe 9 after recipe 8 (vial 13), whose two recipes are only 6.6 apart, so carryover cannot be seen in colour there.
